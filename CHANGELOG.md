# What's new in Kick Drops

Versions follow `major.minor.patch`. Each release's notes are written when it's published.

## 0.1.0 (current)
- First version: emote and badge drop farming with Kick's own player, auto-claim, moving on between categories, and the drop queue (drag to reorder)
- Live dashboard with a forecast chart, live monitor, ETAs and session stats, sized to fit a maximized window
- Recovery from stalls, offline or crashed streams, category changes and sleep, plus an alarm when another stream on your account takes the credit
- History: a permanent log with an overview, per-drop views, timeline, watch sessions and channels
- Menu bar / tray mode with a progress ring icon and popover
- Encrypted login storage, optional forget-on-quit
